The Story

Soul-searching blues,
straight from the heart

Dave Spaulding has been living and breathing blues for decades. Whether behind the keys — coaxing a B3 Hammond to scream or finding the stark, plaintive voice of a piano — or out front with an electric or acoustic guitar, Dave plays whatever the song calls out to be heard.

Old favorites you know by heart sit alongside Dave's own originals: songs that reach out and speak to the soul of anyone who's ever needed the blues to pull them back up into the light. With a rotating cast of talented players filling out the band, every night is its own thing.
